Research safeguards

  • Untrusted observations are inspected, never executed.
  • The canary is fixed and inert by design.
  • Audio playback requires an explicit user action.
  • Measurements do not imply successful reception or decoding.
Authorized environments only. Use equipment and systems you own or are explicitly permitted to assess. This research node is not a production containment boundary and must not be exposed to untrusted networks without a separate security review.
